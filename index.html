Philosophy
01

Write business logic.
Not boilerplate.

Every project we build starts from the same principle: complexity should be absorbed by tools, not by developers. Focus on solving business problems — not rewriting the same infrastructure for the hundredth time.

02

Ship products.
Not architecture diagrams.

If your 200-line microservice needs 47 layers of abstraction, bounded contexts, and a domain expert consultation — you have different problems. We build for engineers with deadlines, not conference slides.

03

Opinionated
by design.

These aren't suggestions — they're the result of years of frustration. Use what works for you. If you disagree, that's fine. We don't mass care.
